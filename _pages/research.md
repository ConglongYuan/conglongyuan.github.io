        <h2 id="order-title">What selects a soft ordered state?</h2>

    <section id="defects-boundaries" class="research-topic" aria-labelledby="defects-title">
      <div class="research-topic__copy">
        <p class="studio-label">02 / Defects &amp; Boundaries</p>
        <h2 id="defects-title">How do boundaries organize a texture?</h2>
        <p>Interfaces and patterned illumination provide ways to organize liquid-crystal defects and surface reliefs. My published work uses pixelated holographic lithography to program smectic defect arrangements and the structures they produce.</p>
        <p class="research-question">An ongoing question: how do topology and boundary conditions constrain which textures can be reconfigured?</p>
        <ul class="research-papers" aria-label="Selected publications on defects and boundaries">

      <div class="research-topic__copy">
        <p class="studio-label">03 / Soft Photonics</p>
        <h2 id="photonics-title">How can soft order shape light?</h2>
        <p>Helical structures and patterned liquid-crystal elements connect material organization to spectra, polarization, and wavefronts. Published examples range from stable heliconical photonics to structured-light generation with liquid-crystal geometric-phase polymers.</p>
