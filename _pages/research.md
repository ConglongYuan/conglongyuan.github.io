## Elastic Physics of Soft Matter

Phase evolution, elastic competition, and the formation and control of heliconical, defect, and boundary structures in liquid-crystal systems.

## Biomedical Optics and Instrumentation

Multimodal computational optical imaging, label-free biological imaging, and optical micromanipulation instrumentation.

## Optical-Field Control and Optical Communications

Liquid-crystal optical elements for controlling amplitude, phase, polarization, and spatial modes, with interests in structured light and turbulence-resilient optical communications.
