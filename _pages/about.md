I am a lecturer in the **School of Physics, East China University of Science and Technology**.

My research focuses on the physical behavior and optical functions of liquid-crystal and soft-matter systems, with particular interests in:

- **Elastic physics of soft matter**
- **Biomedical optics and instrumentation**
- **Liquid-crystal-based optical-field control and optical communications**

This website provides a concise record of my research interests, selected publications, research projects, patents, and academic experience.
